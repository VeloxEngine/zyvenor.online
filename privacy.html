Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

At TechAI Insights, protecting your digital privacy and maintaining absolute transparency regarding your personal information is paramount. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and safeguard information when you interact with our website and publications.

1. Information We Collect

We deliberately minimize data collection to what is essential for delivering high-performance research content:

  • Directly Provided Information: When you subscribe to our research newsletter or submit a research inquiry via our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message content.
  • Technical Telemetry: Standard, anonymized HTTP log information including approximate geographic region, browser type, referral URLs, and requested assets to optimize global edge CDN distribution.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected telemetry and subscriber data strictly for:

  • Delivering requested research dispatches and architectural newsletters.
  • Diagnosing latency issues and monitoring cyber-security integrity.
  • Responding directly to inquiries initiated through our contact form.

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to data brokers or third-party advertising networks.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We avoid invasive cross-site tracking cookies. Any cookies utilized are strictly technical and temporary—aimed at remembering your interface preferences and ensuring smooth navigation.

4. Your Data Rights

Regardless of your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to the data we hold regarding you, request corrections, or request immediate deletion from our newsletter index. To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@techai-insights.com or use our contact page.
